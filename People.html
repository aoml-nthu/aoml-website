Current Centre Members

Group Leader

Prof. Hao-Wu Lin (林皓武 教授)

Hao-Wu Lin graduated from National Taiwan University in 2002 with a bachelor's degree in electrical engineering. He received his Ph.D. degree from the Graduate Institute of Photonics and Optoelectronics, National Taiwan University in 2007. After his Ph.D. studies and military service, he joined AU Optronics Corp. for research and development on organic light-emitting displays. In 2009, he joined the faculty of National Tsing-Hua University in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering. His current research interests include organic semiconductors for optoelectronic and electronic devices, next-generation photovoltaics, flat panel displays, and nanoscience and technologies.
